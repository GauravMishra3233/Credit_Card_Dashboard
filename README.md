# Credit Card Transaction & Customer Insights Dashboard 📊

An interactive, end-to-end Power BI dashboard designed to provide a comprehensive 360-degree view of credit card performance, revenue streams, and customer demographics. This project transforms raw financial data into actionable business strategies for credit card issuers looking to maximize revenue and improve customer engagement.

## 🚀 Live Visuals & Dynamic Interaction

The project consists of two core report pages built in Power BI Desktop. The interactive cross-filtering, slicing capabilities, and real-time canvas updates are fully demonstrated in the project video (`Screen Recording 2026-07-02 075628.mp4`).

## 📊 Key Insights & Analytics

### 1. Financial Performance (`Screenshot 2026-06-09 014234.png`)
* **Core Metrics:** Instantly monitors business health via high-level KPI cards tracking **$57M in Total Revenue**, **$45.5M in Transaction Amounts**, and **$8.0M in Interest Earned**.
* **Card Tier Performance:** The **Blue** card category overwhelmingly dominates the business portfolio, accounting for **$47M** of the total revenue, followed by Silver ($6M), Gold ($3M), and Platinum ($1M).
* **Usage Channels:** Customers strongly prefer **Swipe** transactions ($36M) over Chip ($17M) and Online ($4M) methods.
* **Top Spend Categories:** Bill payments ($14M), Entertainment ($10M), and Fuel ($10M) drive the highest transaction revenue.

### 2. Customer Demographics (`Screenshot 2026-06-09 014341.png`)
* **Top Contributing Age Group:** Adults aged **40-50** are the highest revenue-generating cohort ($25M combined), followed closely by the 50-60 age bracket.
* **Education & Profession:** Graduates account for the largest revenue slice ($23M). Professionally, Blue-collar and Business professionals represent the highest customer spend segments.
* **Geographic Distribution:** **Texas (TX)** and **New York (NY)** lead the portfolio in regional transaction revenue.
* **Income & Marital Status:** High-income and married customer groups show a higher density of credit utilization and revenue generation.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Interactive Features
As demonstrated in `Screen Recording 2026-07-02 075628.mp4`, the dashboard utilizes advanced Power BI capabilities to deliver an intuitive user experience:

* **Power BI Desktop & Power Query:** Used for end-to-end data modeling, data transformation, ETL processes, and dashboard canvas design.
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Used to build dynamic KPIs, time-intelligence metrics, and customized customer segmentation buckets (e.g., Income Group and Age Group levels).
* **Cross-Filtering & Drilldowns:** Clicking any matrix row or visual element instantly cross-filters the remaining charts.
* **Interactive Slicers:** Includes localized top-bar slicers allowing stakeholders to dynamically slice entire reports by **Quarter (Q1-Q4)**, **Week Start Date**, **Gender (M/F)**, **Income Level (Low/Mid/High)**, and **Card Tier**.
